## Headline

**Uncorking the conversations behind ONS 2026**

A brand, a website, a full set of event materials and a LinkedIn presence for Stavanger's new networking pavilion, delivered in about ten weeks.

---

## The challenge

ONS is one of the world's largest energy conferences, bringing an estimated 70,000+ visitors to Stavanger every other year. The exhibition halls are busy and loud. Many of the conversations that actually move business forward happen later, downtown.

For 2026, ONS+ and a group of partners came together to build a co-created pavilion in downtown Stavanger: a place for energy leaders, investors, scaleups and international delegations to meet, showcase their work and do business. The partners voted on a name, and the pavilion became **UnCorked**.

The name created the first design challenge. "Uncorked" suggests popping a bottle of champagne, but Norwegian law strictly limits alcohol advertising. The identity had to capture the feeling of something being released, with no bottles, glasses or drinks anywhere in it.

The brief also asked for:

- A look that was "corporate edgy": energetic and fun, but still professional, and a clear break from the grey, institutional feel of typical conference branding
- A sense of hospitality, like walking into a good hotel lobby
- A logo that could stand on its own and also sit alongside the ONS+ mark
- A website live within days to support event announcements on LinkedIn and Facebook, with a programme that linked to Luma for registrations
- Everything ready in time for 24 August, with the programme and partner list still changing up to the last minute

---

## Our approach

We kept the work fast and lean. Design and development ran in parallel, decisions were made on short calls with the client, and the website was built so the client's team could update text, images and the programme themselves.

### 1. Brand identity: bubbling, not bottled

We explored a wide range of logo directions, from bold block wordmarks to playful scripts and colour combinations. The client chose a clean, confident wordmark with sparkles rising out of it. It suggests energy being released without ever showing what is being opened.

The client also wanted to show what UnCorked was releasing: innovation, connections, technology, the future of energy. Packing all of that into the logo would have made it unreadable at small sizes. Instead we built it into a separate graphic system of sparkles, bursts and patterns that could be used across the website, social posts and print.

**The palette.** We tested four colour schemes on the live website layout. The client chose deep purple (#5D23BC) with electric lime (#C6FE33). It has the energy the brief asked for and is still professional, and it matched the colours already seen in the ONS event photography.

**Typography.** Bricolage Grotesque for headlines and Space Grotesk for body copy: modern, a little quirky, and easy to read on screen and in print.

**Lockups.** The UnCorked wordmark works alone or with the ONS+ logo, with a version that includes a tagline line for campaign use.

### 2. Website: a one-page hub for the whole week

uncorked.no was designed as a single, scrollable page that answers the questions visitors actually have: what is this, what is on, how do I join, and where is it.

Page structure:

- **Hero:** the UnCorked and ONS+ lockup over an official ONS photo of the downtown pavilion area, with two calls to action: *See the program* and *Become a partner*
- **Partner logo strip:** "In partnership with", showing the co-create partners from the first fold
- **The concept:** "Big conversations deserve great meeting places", with four pillars: curated networking, inspiring conversations, showcase opportunities and dynamic programming
- **Programme:** a day-by-day schedule for the event week, with each session linking to Luma for registration, waiting lists and guest approval
- **Become a partner:** an invitation to showcase, speak or sponsor, with a simple *Request a spot* button that opens an email
- **Golden Ticket:** a section explaining how partners' invited guests register using their ticket
- **What to expect:** the atmosphere, the audience and the format
- **News and blog:** built in and kept hidden until there was content to publish
- **Footer:** location and contact

**Built for the client to run.** We developed the site in WordPress with modular sections, so the client's team could edit copy live, swap images, hide or show sections and add programme items without going back to developers. Registrations stayed on Luma, which already handled approvals and waiting lists, so the website stayed simple.

**Getting the word out early.** Before the full site launched, we produced 16:9 event cover images for LinkedIn and Facebook events and a holding page, so promotion could start while the programme was still being finalised.

### 3. On-site experience: event and print design

The pavilion needed to feel like UnCorked from the street to the last drink voucher. We designed the full set of physical materials:

- **"Wall of Fame" partner tent banner:** a large photo wall showing every partner logo, laid out so the logos stay visible when people stand in front of it. Designed in the brand's lime green to work with the pavilion's bohemian tent interior.
- **Entrance banner:** marking the pavilion, which sat at the entry point to the ONS+ downtown area
- **Golden Ticket:** the invitation partners gave to selected guests, designed as a premium gold ticket with a QR code linking to registration. We explored several versions, from art-deco gold to bold poster styles, with and without QR codes.
- **Partner and host badges:** "Your host" badges for the pavilion team and partners
- **Event signage:** wayfinding and information signs around the pavilion
- **"Secret Networking Mission" pamphlet:** a playful icebreaker that gave guests a mission to start conversations
- **Drink vouchers and food menu:** designed in the same identity, while respecting the rules on alcohol marketing
- **Extras:** tent cards, flyers, a roll-up banner, web banners and merchandise mockups (stickers, tote bags, T-shirts, tape) to show how the identity extends

### 4. LinkedIn: building awareness before the doors opened

With about three weeks to go, we created and launched the **ONS+ UnCorked LinkedIn page** and took over the content.

- **Page setup:** profile, banner and brand voice, using the event's official naming and Norwegian sentence-case style
- **Content plan:** a weekly plan for the run-up to the event, focused on building awareness and keeping momentum
- **Pre-event creatives:** a template system for announcements, key information and countdowns, including day posts, "Today's schedule" posts and team and photo posts
- **Speaker spotlights:** "Meet our expert" cards and slides introducing each speaker
- **Partner amplification:** content designed for partners and speakers to share, extending the reach beyond the page's own followers

Every post on the page was planned, designed and published by the Kilowott team.

### 5. Venue video

We produced a video for screens at the pavilion. It introduces the UnCorked brand and concept and showcases the programme, sponsors and pavilion partners, so visitors understood the space and the ecosystem around it as they walked in.
